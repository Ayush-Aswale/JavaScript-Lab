About Smart India Hackathon

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a nationwide initiative organized by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC) and AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education). It provides students with a national platform to solve pressing challenges faced by our daily lives, thereby inculcating a culture of product innovation and a mindset of problem-solving.


SIH is conducted across two flagship editions:

  • Software Edition: A 36-hour non-stop digital product development competition.
  • Hardware Edition: A 5-day continuous product prototyping and fabrication challenge.

Students collaborate in diverse multidisciplinary teams to design scalable, cost-effective, and deployable tech solutions for various Ministries, Departments, and Premier Industry Partners.
